**Shawn Weekly** is a Principal Solution Architect, software engineer, and independent researcher with more than twenty years of experience across electrical utility design systems, enterprise software architecture, IEC CIM data modeling, knowledge graphs, and applied AI/ML.

*Note: Writing, open-source tooling, and independent research published on cimbology.com represent my own views and work.*

## Focus Areas

- **IEC CIM in Design Workflows:** Bringing IEC 61970 and IEC 61968 out of operations-only exports into open, design-time semantic graphs.
- **CIMBOLOGY Graph:** An open semantic graph approach combining canonical IEC CIM, minimal design-stage physical and P&C extensions, W3C SHACL validation profiles, declarative RDF lenses, and JSON-LD.
- **Open-Source Tooling:** Creator and maintainer of [`dotTC57`](https://github.com/s-o-w/dotTC57), an MIT-licensed .NET implementation of IEC TC57 CIM with a `.NET 10` OWL 2 ontology generator.
- **Graph-Grounded AI:** Hybrid Knowledge Graph + RAG (GraphRAG) systems where deterministic graph queries, SHACL validation, and explicit citations anchor every model response.
