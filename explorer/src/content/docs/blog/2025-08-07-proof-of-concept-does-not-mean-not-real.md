*Adapted from my August 2025 post on utility engineering POCs.*

In the utility sector, "proof of concept" often gets treated as polite shorthand for a science experiment: interesting to watch in a meeting, then shelved while everyone goes back to spreadsheets and 2D drawings. That mindset holds the industry back.

A serious engineering POC is neither half-baked nor throwaway code. When built with clean boundaries, typed schemas, and automated verification from the start, a prototype evolves directly into production software without throwing the foundation away.

## Why So Much Utility Graph and AI Work Starts as a POC

Most early knowledge-graph and grounded-AI tooling in power delivery starts in POC form for organizational reasons, rarely technical ones:

- Uncertainty around generative AI reliability and governance
- Legitimate safety sensitivity around power-delivery workflows
- Institutional inertia and *"we have always done it this way"*

Waiting for a five-year enterprise consensus before writing a single line of working software guarantees another decade of duct-taped workflows.

## Show Working Proof, Then Measure It

Early systems built on [IEC CIM](/cim/), RDF triplestores, and grounded retrieval already answer real engineering questions, connect real asset data, and surface verifiable citations. Putting working prototypes in front of engineers and measuring them honestly, as in the [CIMBOLOGY GraphRAG PE exam benchmark](/research/cimbology-graphrag/), builds far more trust than another slide deck promising future transformation.
